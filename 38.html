Cool and Easy Feeling

That cool and easy feeling that follows you around in life that usually precedes times to rest and recollect everything, like the mornings dew resting on the skin and the lungful of air that wakes you in the morning. The room still there still the same as when you slept, but different, you are different always and every day different. The darkness that envelopes you, not to destroy or erase but to protect and keep you forever in its loving embrace "i'll join you all soon, wait for me!" The Moon in perfect allignment with venus and some other heaenly body or satellite can remind you of the truly wonderful state that humanity was created from, Creation was all we every needed and the creation we created from Creation is so obviously flawed and corupt, but I am reminded perfect too! The same smell that two very different people can have reminds us that we are all together forever in love. Enemies and strangers walk together in love and similartude forever in the darkness forever closed around on all sides, forever and the candle is blown out forever.
