Work of God

"Jesus answered and said unto them, this is the work of God, that ye believe on Him whom He hath sent." (John 6:29) I certainly might be the guiltiest when it comes to passing the buck, or givin up. Often I say well that doesn't really matter or that isn't really MY responsibility, and this always ends up in nothing getting done. I think what I'm excetional at is defining what is not my job, but I'm perhaps poor in understanding what is my job. God created humanity to share in the joys of creation, but because of our fallen state we must also work towards the greater joy, so what is humanity's work? I have no real answer but only to see what Jesus did here on Creation; to be gentle and meek and to reach out to those around Him. Lately I've been having difficult thoughts about the purpose and ends of the Church here as we see it, and I think that these top down organizations always devolve into something resembling corporate America, only naturally because they structure themselves that way, and in turn they are entirely interested in the things that corporate America is interested in, money, butts in pews, influence, power, and innovation. But it strikes me that this is no where in the teachings of Christ, in fact we often see the opposite. I believe that if we outsource the work of God to the clergy, we also outsource the mission and identity of Christianity to people who are more interested in organization than mission. If every church in the world were shuttered and a Mass was never said again, the teaching and mission of Christ would still be true, perhaps more so! The coming of Christ wasn't to say there will be some small priestly class who is godly and like me, but instead it was to say that everyone is like God, everyone contains God in their inner most being, and as such must take up the works of God as their most basic driving force. We must stop allowing so called 'professional' ministers to hijack the mission that Jesus gives to us in the Gospels. The longer we outsource our duty, the more damage will be done.

-Alex
