God's Loneliest

God's lonliest stars in the sky, look into the dark early morning sky and the stars pierce like light in the infinite no thing, the Heavenly bodies the sky the sky the chill and the sky. The little bit of green and red like salmon swimming up in the infinite no thing nothing prething the sky. The sky that I look upward to every morning to remember that I can love and that love is material and real the sky that eases my fear of death and that brings me closer to the divine. The sky made early by God and shown to humans forever, I'm jealous of the early humans that lived without science who could see the heavenly bodies for as they were, high and distant bodies which were the hope to be, to be heavenly bodies as a reward for life in the earthyly bodies, that is what the stars are! Don't believe the science of many suns as there is just one sun and one son. The heavenly bodies that hang above us an give us direction. The heavenly bodies, but look the stars have faded and fled away but one hangs there in the sky and burns brightly! How brightly it leads me and calls to me - maybe Venus! Perhaps Jupiter, but no matter, Heavenly body that hangs on through the night and into the morning! It is love that hangs on like that in the sky and I begin to pray because already my heart is hard and stone like but this heavenly body guides me toward the day and toward the son.
