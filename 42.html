September 24, 2025

Cat

Today I hope for the ability to live life as my cat does, to be excited for the banality of life. to eat the same food from a can day after day and to eat with such fervor (she is quite fat), to be so joyous when she hears my car pull up as though we had not seen each other in 5 years and not 5 hours, or to rush to an open door or window and enjoy the cool breeze with the sunbeam saturating her with morning light. It seems so obvious to me that animals share in the sentience and joy and anguish of Creation just as much as anyone, although animal life is a pyramid with humanity at the top as protector, we are also very much a part of this beatly pyramid. Humanity's arrogance has done serious damage to the part of us that is simple and without ego like the animals, the part that can truly feel joy in the day to day of life. Lifes tragic beauty comes and goes over me like so many waves in the ocean, but the sweetness of life and breath in my lungs will always drive me onward. Today, I will live with some simple joy as the beasts of the field.

-Alex
