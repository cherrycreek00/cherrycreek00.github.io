Breath

The bottle of wine was uncorked and place on the counter and it sit there. The wine was breathing and filling the air with wine notions, the walls breated too and the room swelled and contracted and I felt everything breathing, except for me, I don't remember that I was breathing. I can't remember too how the wine was poured, but the wine was poured in a cheap drinking glass and it was filled almost to the brim so that a little bit of wine poked above the rim, and the tension kept it from spilling over, "this do ye, as oft as ye drink it, in remembrance of me." (1 Cor 11:25). The wine was red and inky and thickly red like blood like that blood that beat hard in my head and filled up the vials. When it had touched my lips the wet dry taste refreshed me lay in my belly like fire. The bottle still sat there breathing and staring and the light shined through the bottle in transparent green-ness. This little bit of wine will gladden my heart, I thought this little bit of wine will be for freedom and it all washed away - everything washed away in warmness and life. Just glass fulls of life that go down and lay inside you to make you forget wish you might want to remember and to bring to you mind those things which you might have forgotten.

-Alex
