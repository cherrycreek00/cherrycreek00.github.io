Time

Time slips through our hands like so many ribbons fluttering in the wind, they are divided and they are no more and should we try to gather it all up, we find that it has only aged us and we can no longer run to pick up the pieces. Sometimes it is pay day and when I blink it is payday again and I've worked all week and I cannot remember it. Too often my life is just an inconvienent reality between big moments in my life. I'm famous of this, just biding my time. I want more for life to happen every day and for the big things to be built upon big life - it only makes sense for everything to be as big as it can be because one day you are to become infinitely little. So be big in everything you do, in this life love as bigly as you can, and enjoy the fruits of Creation which God put there for you to enjoy. Feel the passion that you heart constantly emanates, but we must ignore. Pursue those passions so far as they make life worth living. Eschew anything which seperates you from the enjoyment of life because 80 or 90 or 100 years is nothing when compared to infinity. Humanity is constantly gazing at infinity, but forced to be in the temporal, but if we live and live and live, we bring some of that eternal essence to the here and the now.

~Alex
