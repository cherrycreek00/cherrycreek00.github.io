Sleep

To sleep is to satisfy the most basic human need, but also to put your hands on the height of human mystical experice at once. Many times in my life the way out of a difficult situation has been handed to me through dreams and yet others I was unable to think clearly because of a lack of sleep. To sleep is to enter unto another world that is similar enough to ours, but at the same time radically different. Paradoxically, entering into this other world leaves us feeling refreshed and ready to live in that baser of worlds. It is like a journey that at the end leaves you more ready to embark than when you set off!

"like a dreamless sleep, then death would be a great advantage. For I think that if one had to pick out that night during which a man slept soundly and did not dream, put beside it the other nights and days of his life, and then see how many days and nights have been better and more pleasant than that night" - Socrates

"And it shall come to pass afterward That I will pour out My Spirit on all flesh; Your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, Your old men shall dream dreams, Your young men shall see visions." (Joel 2:28)

-Alex
