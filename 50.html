October 9, 2025

Travelogue Part 2

I read Hemingway's 'A Farewell to Arms' and like it decently. I don't think that any of the usual criticism of Hemingway are that valid or really interesting enough to be worth talking about, but what I did see in him is a profound lack of the love of living, or at least that's what his writing feels enginered to convey. To say I am in the world, but that isn't so neat to me, but I will live life too! But I think that some exceptional moments of beauty stand out like the lightning quick and cutting descriptions and some few neat descriptions of places in Italy.

. . .

The world is beautiful and all the people only enhance that and even sometimes I think that the true beauty of Creation is in the people and this world exists only to spread it all around. One beer is to calm my frayed nerves and two beers is to give me the confidence that I need, three beers is to make everyone your friend and four beers will only cause you troubles. I float around as a little fool and an innocent child with nothing but my Bible and I ask everyone questions and questions and questions and I'm floating and everyone gentle leads me to where I need to go. And I sleep.

. . .

I wake up twice to go run to the bathroom and throw up, and the third time I can't make it and just throw up between my legs. "excuse me, the young man beside me throws up on the floor." It's funny and beautiful beyond words, only I feel bad to throw up on her shoes and to make someone else clean it up, but being sick on a plane is tenderness and the Godliness of people and if I could get a little food in me I'll be right as rain.

-Alex
