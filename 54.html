October 22, 2025

Thoughts

"Too much and too little wine. Give Him none, he cannot find truth. Give him too much, the same thing Men spend their time chasing a ball or a hare. It is the entertainment even of kings." - Blaise Pascal

It's beautiful in my mind the way that a rainy morning can bring back all sort of memories, something I might be guilty of is ruminating on the past more than I ought, but it is undeniable that memories are sweeter than real life, and the hope of the future life better than working to make this one more enjoyable. Even times in which I lived in anguish, when viewed from the warm comfort of my rocking chair are pleasant and dreamy. One particular memory about a short affair with a tragic woman haunts me especially. The long walks in the biting wind through snow and disreputable neighborhoods are transformed to something lovely and pleasant and in my mind it makes sense now because they just prefigured the stifling warmth in her apartment. Perhaps I shouldn't go any further because I hope to one day attach my person to this website and I hope to not give too much of my personal life! But only that this morning I chase the hare, unable to read because my mind does not wish to read the lives and tales of others, but is hungry for the story of itself! What better story than one you lived. Maybe as activity in my life decreases, the desire to go back to the interesting parts of my life increases. I'm glad that I have all these memories and I hope one day I can write them all down in a complete way and share them with you.

-Alex
