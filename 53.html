Fever

It's all feverish in color, the brght red bouces off the steel all around in the dark and you can see it there haunting and malicious, and the redness too conveys a sense of heat and fever and sweat and moisture that builds all over until it comes unto you all of a sudden at once and the fever has taken full control of you. And from somewhere far off the icy winds blows against the windowpane and makes water on the window, enough to assuage your thirst...

The trees of Autumn sat redly in the dying day light, an intense golden ray that focused onto the oak tree. I want to leave everything behind just to sit under it's leaves, but the big machinery of life chugs on and careens me past the tree and all that is beautiful, the machinery of life only cares to drive me toward that open grave, and how quickly too!

-Alex
