October 8, 2025

The Observer

Sometimes in life when everything seems to be overwhelming, I want to take on this role that I call the observer. Some one who exists just to see life, but not to participate or to be seen by life. Seeing life happen is so very dear to me, but for me, humans are the scariest thing on the planet, and just when I think that I understand them, I find that I'm not at all where I thought I was. Humanity is incredible though because it is nothing less than a portion of the living God. The fullness of God saw it fit to send His only son to become a part of humanity, to live admist humanity in it's entirety. I believe that Jesus saw this too, in His draw to save the world, but also the way that the world wearied Him greatly and the many such scenes of Jesus sneaking away to some hidden mountain top to be along. It's that desire to float up just high enough to be above, but still a part - an intermediary space. For about two years, I was deeply interested in Quaker worship and would every Sunday go to a Quaker meeting (a grand old 19th century meeting house with an ancient oak tree and massive cemetery behind). When I was well in practice, I could feel my spirit leave my body and hover just above my head - being drawn out upward toward God. This is only to say that there is something of being alone with people that is special in God's eyes. My attraction to humanity is the greatest driver in my life, but the complexity scares me - interacting with humanity is nothing less than comeing to a more perfect knowledge of God.

-Alex
