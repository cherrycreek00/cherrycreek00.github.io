September 27, 2025

Poem

Clothes hung out to dry, on hangers stuck in the gutter in the damp morning air. There is a dull pain all around that hangs and floats about penetrating everything, soaking deep into the deck so much so that everything slants and bends toward the sun, the new day the feel of warmth and the smell of spice and sewage. It is a joy to feel everything in life even the dull pain of the mind and the pain in the knees and the pain in the ankle, the stiffness in the back it is all only so much to feel and remind you of the great multitude that this life is. The sun comes up and everything is sticky with humid yesterday.

-Alex
