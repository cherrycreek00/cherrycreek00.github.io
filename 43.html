The Rain

The sound of the rain was the only sound that was there when I woke up, it was the sound of the rain hititng the roof and the side walk, and puddles of water. There wasn't any other sound or anyone to make any other sounds and the rain dominated and pounded, my first thought was that the sound of rain is peaceful, and people who love watching it rain usually sleep in warm dry beds. Outside I felt the door knob and it was sticky with the rain and already it was humid and my clothes stuck to me, the sharp smell of decay and the herb garden were sharpened by the moisture, and I drove to work with my windows down because I wanted to feel the rain and know how it is to be wet. On the way to work I saw two people sleeping under the church awning with their two dogs, large dogs, maneaters. I gave the dogs treats and asked if they needed anything and told them they should leave once the rain lets up, "yes the doctors say they can reattach the arm, but unfortunately not in my lifetime" As I left work the clouds parted and the sun steamed the ground so the blacktop steamed upward to heaven. I was looking for a rainbow, but only the blinding light that shone down hard on the redbrick and the mountains far in the distance. Everything was wet and the wetness penetrated my mind and I felt like my soul was damp, and I frowned at the world knowing that I should be happy. Rainy days are beautiful when you are close to someone you love and warm and dry, when you never leave the house, but stay inside all day to read, glancing up and seeing the pitter patter sometimes hard and sometimes just spitting, the rain makes the world peripheral and someone else's problem. "And the waters prevailed, and were increased greatly upon the earth (Gen 7:18)

-Alex
