Poem

The wind came in billows and filled up the landscape with wind. The pastoral landscape that was the basis of all other landscapes from which he would look and compare, who thought the entire world must look like this, but not when when wind came howling down the valley and everything was filled with movement like possessed with spirits or filled with little squirels. It was his hands that always got him in trouble he couldn't control them. Those same hands which could reach out to hold or embrace or touch the world are the hands that can destory and hurt. The hands that get him in trouble, but when he looked at them they looked like everyone else's hands no different but all his life he heard they were different and evil, and his mother said it was never his fault, and if the world was just mother he wouldn't have any problem, but the world is evil, and causes him trouble, more trouble than his hands could ever cause... he couldn't deal with all the evil so he went a drinkin and was out of the way. He went a drinkin and like to drive to forget to pretend and plan to run away, but every time his escape route went right back home because as much as his hands could hurt, he was hurt and scared and only the pastoral landscape that he knew and pressed against every memory in his mind could calm him down. And the trees turned red and golden in the cold and the wind blew the leaves all over and the drinkin came to take him away.

-Alex
