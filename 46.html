God's Loneliest People

God's loneliest people, in the rain and humid sticky air walking the same worn path and same threadbare clothing and same immovable smile. Who doth rest his head upon the decaying leaves of the woods, sleeps and dreams visionary dreams. Completing his rounds to do them again, to force out all darkness in the park and to be God's loving embrace. God's loneliest people well read and patient to sit under a tree and wait for marching orders, to march up unto the hill and look down on God's domain and smile to know it isn't his and never will be.

~Alex
