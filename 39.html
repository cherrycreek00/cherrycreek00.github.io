Sky Full of Rain

Sometimes when the sky is full of rain and you are bound inside, your mind wll take the rains and lead you to where you'd rather not go - as though a certain amount of movement is required out of humanity either physically or mentally, so that walking down the street or in your mind it is indistinguishable, everything real as your mind decides what is real and what you brush aside as fiction. And when the sky is full of rain and raining buckets you mind will take you by the arm and show you things you once saw. It will take you to a loft, small and foreign just 12x12 cluttered but warm so warm in fact it's hot and sweating in winter clothes. The loft where the snow always falls just outside the window and you spend an entire winter watching the snow fall on pristine streets, undisturbed, wondering where humanity has gone but it is only too cold and humanity is holed up in lofts that trap warm air and get hot! That loft where you must duck so not to hit your head as though built not for you, but someone specific, someone 5'1" who could happily live there forever, but you are an intruder - truly and forever different constantly alert and listening for the proper tenant to come crunching down the snow, or loud pipes to come screaming through the snow, only they never come and the snow keeps falling ontop of snow and no one crunches it except you when you leave through and walk to where you ought to be.

-Alex
