Dread

Sometimes a dreadful feeling will comes around in the warmth of bed and siezes me in my warm and restful lulling presleep, and I too scared to think oh what could be the casuse, I turn to turn away from this dread, my body too scared to begin a search for the cause of my dread, everything in life causes me dread. I might be the guiltiest conscience in the world for as much as I worry, but why worry? Is not all of this dread I feel just foolishness? Can not I just think away this dread and agony, my lamentations turned to joy! It is my choice to rejoice or to be depressed in this world, as it is everyone elses choice. We live in this Creation that is half created for humanitys rejoicing, and half just for tricking us into a deeper depresssion. I want to hold on always to the joy in life and never to the dread. Even the greatest dread can not seperate me from the greatest joy of all creation, only my mind can do that only my flaws and human weakness can trick me to believe that the world is against me! When really God is for me and if God is for me, who can be against me?

-Alex
