October 4, 2025

Poem

Heaven's bow pulled taut and shot the sunrise into the sky. It was turquoise and emerald like water that made you want to dive in and forget about the world below. The sky was like a lake or maybe the Atlantic Ocean with little long and red sandy barrier islands that made you want to jump in and start anew, wandering those islands for as long as you could. To look up at the land and know that whatever foolishness that existed there wasn't your foolishness any longer but someone else's. A distant other world where people fought and rushed around with vanity, but on the islands in the sky there was a cool breeze and you lay into the sand and close your eyes.

~Alex
