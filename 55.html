October 26, 2025

To Recall

"I am weary with my groanings; all the night make I my bed to swim I water my couch with my tears." (psalm 6:6)

We say together with David all the night I make my bed to swim and this notion here is one of the strongest that I feel in all of scripture. To me David is lamenting the human ability to recall. I don't know how universal this is, but I feel every action and I weigh them as I try and sleep - many nights I lay in my bed and think through my life and my mistakes (and occasionally I will think beautifully of good and peaceful times that ended happily and I will go to sleep well), and a heat will overwhelm me and my room is filled with an ancient and suffocating fire. This is it, the way in which our minds magnify everything every memory thought and dream into something that takes control. Weary with groanings! Only God can take away this feeling, and often the only way I can sleep is by repeating the Jesus prayer as my bulwark against sundry thoughts in my bed. The Psalms occupy such a large place in my heart because it shows to every successsive generation that these things you feel aren't new and the battles you fight have been waged for thousands of years, so take heart that we have God, for as David says, "Depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity; for the LORD hath heard the voice of my weeping. The LORD hath heard my supplication; the LORD wil receive my prayer." (Psalm 6:8-9). And this is what I always forget, that the LORD will hear my weeping and will answer my prayer, only I never ask because I think that it is my problem to solve and it has come about through some weekness on my own part, but this is arrogance and pride and real change and real strength of spirit only comes through relinquishing everything and in my low desperate state i say to God please have mercy on me. Like the publican who would not even look up, but smote his breast. Knowing that to sin is the conditon of man and to overcome that is the conditon of God. I will never be perfect until I am with God again, but I am given little opportunities to bring about God in this world in little ways, to be merciful, loving, and contrite. And all good things flow forth from a contrite heart. To know that sickness and anguish is my lot and use it to reach ever out toward my God. To sit and take a breath away form the rushing around of the world. To know that the sunrise is a sign from God that His Son will come again in just this way, that God loves the earth and all it's creatures, and brings the sun to shine on the rightetous and the sinner alike.

-Alex
