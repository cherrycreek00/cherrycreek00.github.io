October 10, 2025

Travelogue Part 1

... Sometimes humans desperation for food is overwhelming and it's good to see that everything doesn't matter as much as getting to Del Taco and getting just enough food to feel right as rain.

I was supposed to drive, but I feel so terrible and sick because I threw up all my life force so I begged just a few moments to get myself together and then I could drive. So I played the fool beautifully (something I often do) and it was wonderful! Sometimes the person everyone needs is the fool, a sweet and beautiful fool to gladen hearts, but I wasn't truly the fool they needed because i fell asleep.

In Amarillo I drove and I drove long and hard, but steadily and as quickly as mad sense. Everyone else slept and I saw the sun rise in Arkansas. The ombre in the sky from warm newness of day to the cold reaches of infinity, like someone has shaked a jar and all the sentiments were seperating themselves. This part of the country was clean and made me feel at peace.

I really don't remember much, but so long as there was road under me I kept on driving and sometimes stop to get fuel or food and mostly coffee and then I would keep driving. To think that you can point your car in one direction and just go until you're all the way across and gone inches on the map. I kept thinking that I am somewhere and this is home for many people and what sort of folks lived here or what sort lived there and it made me sad because I'll never know, and I saw all the churches and it was a nice feeling to know that this universal trait of worship that is the throughline of humanity is truly everywhere you look.

We got to Nashville sometime after the sun had sunk down low beyond the hills and stopped into a bar. Nashville had the biggest affect on me of anywhere. I hadn't been since I was a young man (and then I was more interesting in spiting someone than to really enjoy the trip I was on), but this time I dug everything! All the people and all the life that everything exuded. Everything leaped and jumped to the very beat of life. Nashville is a place where you can reach out to someones soul and they'll be reaching right back. I don't care, the soul of America lives down south. And after this there was more road and the darkness got intensely dark and the road just kept going and going.

-Alex
