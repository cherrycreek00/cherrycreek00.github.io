November 3, 2025

Some Thoughts

When I realize the great simplicity and perfection of God's creation, it gives me a feeling of completion. The sense that I cannot nor do I want to create anything tiny in this world, only to just to experience it is the fullness of this life. The sound of footsteps on the back porch, wood creaking, to watch the clouds float past the bulk and brightness of the moon for hours. Feeling the warm night air upon your skin! There is no work of man that can approach this, and the realization of this fact has brought me a little closer to peace. To sit and to write is no longer a big and important task beacuse God in His perfection will blow it all away into something greater.

-Alex
